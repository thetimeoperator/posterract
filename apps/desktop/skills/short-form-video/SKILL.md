# Short-form Video

A vertical short built from footage and text: fast cuts on the beat, a title card, bold captions, a call to action at the end.

## Workflow

1. Read the scene with posterract_get_context and posterract_read_source.
2. Plan the beats of the video in a short brief before editing.
3. Build the scene with the semantic tools or posterract_write_source, keeping stable element ids.
4. Validate with posterract_validate and posterract_check; inspect posterract_capture before claiming the result.
