# Talking-head short

- Scene: 1080×1920, one exportable video.
- Primary sequence: speaker footage and synchronized audio.
- Secondary sequence: punch-ins or B-roll at explicit time windows.
- Captions: safe-area constrained and checked at multiple timestamps.
- Inspector variables: accent color, headline, caption scale.

Probe the source, render a waveform, inspect a filmstrip, assemble the primary cut, then add captions and overlays. Capture the hook, a mid-sentence caption, every B-roll transition, and the final frame.
